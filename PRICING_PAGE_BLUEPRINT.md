# Gavior Pricing Page Blueprint

## 1. Purpose

The pricing page should help visitors understand:

1. What Gavior offers.
2. Which service or package fits their current stage.
3. The approximate investment required.
4. What is included in that investment.
5. Whether they can purchase a fixed package or need a custom quotation.
6. What they should do next.

The page must cover all Gavior service categories, not only website packages.

> **Pricing approval required:** All prices in this document, except the already-approved ₹999, ₹1,999 and ₹3,999 website packages, are proposed competitive launch prices. They must be reviewed and approved by Gavior before publishing.

---

## 2. Core Pricing Principle

Different services require different pricing models. The page should not force every service into the same type of card.

| Pricing label | Use when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed price | Scope and deliverables are predictable | Gavior Mini — ₹999 |
| Starting from | The service has a clear minimum but varies by scope | Mobile app MVP — from ₹49,999 |
| Monthly | Work continues every month | SEO — from ₹5,999/month |
| Per deliverable | The customer buys one defined output | Video editing — from ₹999/video |
| Custom quote | Requirements can vary significantly | Enterprise applications |

Every non-fixed price must display **“Starting from”** or **“Estimated range.”** This prevents visitors from treating an indicative price as a final quotation.

### Competitive launch-price rule

Gavior should compete through a deliberately limited entry scope, not by selling an undefined service cheaply.

- Display the competitive amount as **“Launch price”** or **“Starting from.”**
- State exactly what the entry scope includes.
- Limit concepts, pages, screens, workflows, integrations and revisions.
- Price additional requirements through documented add-ons.
- Review launch prices after the first 10 customers or after 90 days.
- Never advertise a low price for enterprise, SaaS, AI or cloud work without showing the scope boundary beside it.

## 3. Google Competition Research Summary

Research date: **8 August 2026**. The benchmarks below use publicly visible Indian competitor offers and pricing guides found through Google. Competitor claims are directional market evidence, not independently audited delivery-quality guarantees.

### Website market

- [Rs999.in](https://www.rs999.in/) publicly advertises a complete website package for ₹999.
- [Website999](https://www.website999.in/) positions itself as affordable but advertises website plans starting at ₹7,999.
- Public offers vary sharply between bargain template packages and significantly higher custom-build pricing, so Gavior must separate its fixed website packages from custom development.

**Decision:** Keep Mini at ₹999. Keep Starter at ₹1,999 and Grow at ₹3,999 as aggressive acquisition offers, but define revision, content, domain, hosting and backend limits clearly.

### Marketing and SEO market

- [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/social-media-management-pricing/) reports a typical Indian social-media-management band around ₹15,000–₹35,000/month.
- [Digitoria](https://digitoria.in/home/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/PPC-Advertising-Facebook-Instagram-By-DIGITORIA.pdf) lists social advertising and management packages from ₹11,999/month.
- [Pawan](https://iampawan.in/monthly-seo/) advertises SEO from ₹15,000/month, while broader low-end Indian benchmarks begin near ₹5,000/month for limited freelance scope.

**Decision:** Use ₹5,999–₹9,999 monthly launch prices with strict limits. This sits below many agency packages while remaining more credible than unsustainably cheap unlimited management.

### Branding market

- [DESENO](https://deseno.co.in/branding-cost-india-2026/) places a freelance logo broadly around ₹5,000–₹25,000.
- [CreativeLine](https://creativeline.co.in/blog/logo-design-cost-in-india/) reports basic Indian logo pricing from around ₹2,000, with strategic identity work increasing substantially.
- [Hyper Software](https://www.hypersoftware.in/service/brand-identity-design) places full identity work from established Indian studios broadly around ₹15,000–₹1,50,000.

**Decision:** Introduce a tightly scoped ₹4,999 logo starter and a ₹9,999 brand-identity starter. Do not call the ₹4,999 offer a complete strategic brand identity.

### Product, mobile and SaaS market

- [PS Digitise](https://psdigitise.com/blogs/mobile-app-development-cost-india) advertises React Native mobile-app development starting at ₹75,000.
- [ZoopCoder](https://zoopcoder.com/guides/saas-mvp-cost-in-india.php) places a 2026 Indian SaaS MVP around ₹1,50,000–₹5,00,000.
- [StackPick](https://stackpick.in/mvp-cost/saas) says many real SaaS MVPs need at least ₹3–4 lakh.

**Decision:** Gavior may advertise lower **prototype or micro-MVP** entry prices, but must not describe a ₹74,999 build as an unrestricted production SaaS platform. Full SaaS, mobile and enterprise systems remain custom-scoped.

### AI and infrastructure market

- [Arthat](https://arthat.org/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-automation-agency-cost-in-india) notes that Indian AI-automation pricing varies by workflow scope and integrations.
- [SquareOps](https://squareops.com/blog/devops-consulting-services-cost-pricing-guide-2026/) notes that many DevOps providers avoid public pricing because workload, migration and support scope vary materially.

**Decision:** Publish low-cost audits, setup sprints and single-workflow starters. Keep multi-workflow AI, managed cloud, migrations, high availability and production Kubernetes as custom quotes.

### Positioning conclusion

Gavior should position itself as **“clear-scope, startup-friendly pricing”**, not simply “the cheapest agency.” The public entry price should win the enquiry; the written scope should protect delivery quality and margin.

## 13. What Determines the Final Price

Show six compact explanation cards:

1. **Scope** — Number of pages, screens, workflows or deliverables.
2. **Complexity** — Custom logic, permissions, automation and technical depth.
3. **Integrations** — Payment systems, APIs, CRMs, ERPs and third-party tools.
4. **Content** — Whether copy, media, data entry or migration is required.
5. **Timeline** — Standard delivery versus urgent or phased delivery.
6. **Support** — Handover-only, maintenance or ongoing operational ownership.

Section copy:

`Starting prices make budgeting easier. Your final quotation is confirmed only after Gavior understands the required scope, dependencies and delivery expectations.`

## 15. Delivery and Payment Process

### Step 1 — Choose a starting point

The visitor selects a package, service or consultation.

### Step 2 — Confirm requirements

Gavior reviews goals, deliverables, integrations and timing.

### Step 3 — Approve scope and price

The customer receives a written scope, final quotation and delivery schedule.

### Step 4 — Begin delivery

Work starts after the agreed advance payment.

### Suggested payment terms

These require business approval before publication:

- Small fixed packages: 100% upfront.
- Defined projects: 50% advance, 30% milestone, 20% before launch/handover.
- Monthly services: Paid at the beginning of each billing month.
- Large enterprise projects: Custom milestone schedule.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions

### Are the displayed prices final?

Fixed package prices are final only for the listed scope. “Starting from” prices are estimates and the final quotation depends on confirmed requirements.

### Do website packages include domain and hosting?

No, unless the selected package or proposal explicitly says they are included. Gavior can help purchase and configure them.

### How many revisions are included?

The number of revision rounds should be stated in the selected package or project proposal.

### Can I combine services?

Yes. Gavior can create a combined proposal covering design, development, marketing, AI or infrastructure services.

### Are advertising and cloud costs included?

No. Advertising spend, cloud-provider bills and most third-party subscriptions are separate from Gavior service fees.

### Do you offer monthly support?

Yes. Ongoing design, marketing, development, DevOps and infrastructure support can be structured as a monthly engagement.

### Can I request a custom package?

Yes. If the listed packages do not match the requirement, Gavior will prepare a custom scope and quotation.

### What happens if the scope changes?

Gavior will explain the impact on price and timeline before beginning additional work.

### Do you work with international customers?

Yes. International projects can be quoted in USD or another agreed currency after the scope is discussed.

## 18. Visual Design Direction

The pricing page must remain consistent with the existing Gavior website.

### Use

- White and light-grey page backgrounds.
- Gavior violet (`#7018ff`) for primary emphasis.
- Existing Manrope and DM Sans typography.
- Rounded cards consistent with the Services page.
- Thin grey borders and restrained shadows.
- Dark charcoal only for featured cards and major CTAs.
- Clear spacing between pricing categories.
- Purple highlight for the recommended package in each category.

### Avoid

- A different visual theme from the rest of the website.
- Showing all 30 services as equally large pricing cards.
- Mixing INR and USD in the same pricing section.
- Publishing unapproved prices as fixed commitments.
- Hiding important exclusions in tiny text.
- More than one “Most Popular” card in the same category.
