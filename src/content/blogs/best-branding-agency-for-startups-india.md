The best branding agency for a startup is not automatically the studio that creates the most logo options. It is the partner that can turn a business strategy into a recognisable system your team can use across sales, product, social media and customer communication.

**Disclosure:** Gavior provides brand identity and design services. This guide explains the evidence we believe buyers should use when comparing us with another branding agency.

## Decide what “branding” means for this project

Branding packages can include very different work:

- Positioning and audience definition
- Naming and verbal identity
- Logo design
- Colour and typography system
- Messaging and tone of voice
- Social and sales templates
- Website or product application
- Brand guidelines

A low-cost logo starter should not be presented as a complete strategic brand programme. Ask every provider to separate the thinking, identity creation and applications included in the quote.

## Branding-agency scorecard

| Area | What good evidence looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Business understanding | Audience, category, alternative and desired perception |
| Positioning | A clear, defensible choice—not a list of generic adjectives |
| Distinctiveness | Identity works without copying category conventions blindly |
| Practical application | Tests across website, social, documents and small sizes |
| Accessibility | Colour combinations and typography remain readable |
| Handover | Usable files, licences, templates and guidance |
| Scope clarity | Concepts, revisions, applications and exclusions documented |

## Start with a positioning decision

A startup does not need a long strategy document to make a meaningful choice. It does need clarity on:

1. Who the first customer is.
2. What problem the company is associated with.
3. Which alternatives customers compare.
4. Why the offer is meaningfully different.
5. What the brand should never imply.

Without these decisions, feedback becomes subjective: “make it more premium,” “try another colour,” or “make the logo bigger.”

## Check names before investing heavily

A design agency can perform an initial availability scan, but legal clearance belongs with qualified trademark counsel. India’s [Trade Marks Registry public search](https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/) and WIPO’s [Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/) are useful starting points, not substitutes for legal advice.

Do not approve a complete identity, domain and launch campaign before resolving obvious naming conflicts.

## Test the identity in real conditions

A logo should be reviewed at small sizes, in one colour, on light and dark backgrounds, and beside real content. The broader system should be tested on the applications the startup will actually use: pitch deck, proposal, social post, website header, app icon, invoice or packaging.

Accessibility matters too. The [WCAG 2.2 standard](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) provides contrast and interface criteria for digital use. A brand palette can remain distinctive while defining accessible text combinations.

## Clarify ownership and licences

The handover should state:

- Which final files are supplied.
- Whether editable source files are included.
- Font and stock-asset licence responsibilities.
- Whether unused concepts remain the agency’s property.
- When rights transfer after payment.
- Whether the agency may show the work publicly.

## Red flags

- Unlimited concepts used as the main selling point.
- No audience or positioning discussion.
- Work that only looks convincing in a presentation mockup.
- Fonts or assets supplied without licensing clarity.
- No small-size, monochrome or accessibility checks.
- “Trademark guaranteed” without legal qualification.
- Guidelines that the startup team cannot apply.

## Questions before hiring

1. What strategic decisions happen before visual concepts?
2. Which real applications will be designed?
3. How many concepts and revision rounds are included?
4. How are fonts and stock assets licensed?
5. What source files and guidelines are handed over?
6. How will the identity work accessibly online?
7. What is explicitly outside the package?

The best branding agency gives a startup a system it can recognise, explain and use consistently—not just a logo reveal.
