“Optimise for AI search” is often presented as a new technical discipline. For most B2B websites, it is a simpler and more useful challenge: make the site accessible, specific and credible enough that both people and search systems can understand it.

Google’s own guidance is clear: there are no extra technical requirements or special schema types for appearing as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode. A page must be indexed and eligible to appear with a normal Search snippet. [Google Search Central’s AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) also emphasises crawlability, internal links, useful text and structured data that matches what people can see.

## Start with the basics that are easy to verify

Before producing more content, check these foundations:

1. Important public URLs return `200` to external visitors and search crawlers.
2. `robots.txt` allows the pages you want discovered and links to a sitemap.
3. Each important page has one canonical URL, one clear H1 and a descriptive title.
4. The sitemap contains only public, canonical URLs.
5. Navigation and in-content links make service, case-study and insight pages easy to reach.

These steps do not guarantee ranking or a mention in an AI answer. They remove the avoidable reasons a good page cannot be considered.

## Write the answer before the explanation

On a service page, a visitor should not need to decode a slogan to learn what the company does. Start with a direct answer:

> Gavior designs and builds SaaS products, custom websites, enterprise applications, UI/UX systems and AI workflow automations.

Then add the detail a buyer needs: who the service is for, the business problem, deliverables, process, technology considerations, evidence and next step. This structure is easy to scan and gives search systems complete, grounded context.

## Make evidence visible

The strongest supporting details are specific and reviewable: a named client case study with permission, a real implementation constraint, an architecture diagram, a documented process or an attributed expert perspective. Avoid invented awards, generic “industry-leading” language and unsupported performance figures. Clear limits increase trust.

## Use structured data as confirmation, not decoration

Organization, Service, BreadcrumbList and Article markup can help machines understand visible content. It must reflect the page users see. Markup cannot compensate for thin content, inaccessible pages or a confusing site structure.

## Measure the right signal

Track index coverage, non-brand impressions, qualified organic visits, conversions and the pages that earn meaningful engagement. Search Console reports traffic from Google’s AI features within standard web search reporting, so combine it with analytics and sales feedback rather than chasing a separate “AI ranking” number.
