The best digital marketing agency for a small business is not the team that promises the most impressions, followers or first-page rankings. It is the partner that connects a defined audience and offer to measurable enquiries, appointments or sales—and explains what it can and cannot control.

**Disclosure:** Gavior provides digital marketing, SEO, content and creative services. The scorecard below is designed to compare our plans with any other agency proposal.

## Begin with one commercial goal

Examples of useful goals include:

- Qualified WhatsApp enquiries for a local service
- Booked consultations for a B2B company
- Online purchases for a product category
- Demo requests for a SaaS product
- Repeat visits and branded search demand

“Grow social media” is not yet a business outcome. Define the customer action, its approximate value and how the team will confirm lead quality.

## Marketing-agency scorecard

| Area | Weight | Evidence to request |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Audience and offer | 20 | Customer, problem, promise and reason to act |
| Channel choice | 15 | Why the selected channel fits intent and budget |
| Measurement | 20 | Conversion definitions, tracking and CRM handoff |
| Creative and content | 15 | Formats, volume, approval process and learning plan |
| Optimisation | 15 | Testing rhythm and decision rules |
| Commercial clarity | 15 | Management fee, ad spend, tools and exclusions |

## Choose channels based on intent

- Search advertising can capture existing demand.
- SEO and useful content can build discoverability over time.
- Social media can support familiarity, proof and community.
- Email can nurture known contacts when consent and relevance are clear.
- Retargeting can reconnect with previous visitors, subject to privacy and platform rules.

A small budget spread across every channel often produces too little evidence to learn. A focused first channel with a clear landing page and conversion path is usually easier to evaluate.

## Track actions that matter

Google Ads defines a conversion as a valuable action such as a purchase, signup, phone call or download, and its [conversion-tracking guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6308) explains why measurement connects campaign interactions to business results.

Before launch, agree on:

- Primary conversion
- Secondary engagement signals
- Lead-quality feedback
- Duplicate and spam handling
- Call and WhatsApp attribution limits
- Reporting source of truth

Clicks and reach can diagnose delivery. They are not substitutes for business outcomes.

## SEO cannot be guaranteed

An agency can improve crawlability, content quality, internal linking and relevance. It cannot guarantee a specific organic ranking. Google recommends [helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), not pages written only to repeat target keywords.

Ask how content will include first-hand evidence, expert review, sources and a genuinely complete answer.

## Understand the real monthly cost

Separate:

- Agency management fee
- Advertising spend
- Creative production
- Landing-page work
- Influencer or media fees
- Paid tools and stock assets
- Tax

Gavior’s [marketing pricing](/pricing) shows entry management fees, but final deliverable volume, platforms and ad spend are confirmed in the proposal.

## Account ownership matters

Your company should own advertising accounts, analytics properties, Search Console, social pages, pixels/tags and primary billing access. Give the agency partner access rather than building the business inside an account you cannot recover.

## Red flags

- Guaranteed number-one rankings.
- Reports focused only on reach and followers.
- Ad spend mixed into the agency fee without clarity.
- No landing-page or conversion discussion.
- No way to return lead-quality feedback.
- Agency-owned advertising and analytics accounts.
- Identical content posted across unrelated clients.

## Questions before selecting an agency

1. Which business action will the campaign optimise?
2. Why is this channel appropriate now?
3. Who owns every account and data source?
4. How is lead quality returned to the campaign team?
5. Which costs sit outside the management fee?
6. What will be tested each month?
7. What would cause the agency to change or stop the plan?

The best digital marketing agency makes spend, decisions and outcomes visible. It does not hide uncertainty behind activity reports.
