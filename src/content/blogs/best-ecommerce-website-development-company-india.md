The best e-commerce website development company is the one that understands the operation behind the storefront. Product pages and checkout matter, but so do inventory, fulfilment, returns, taxes, catalogue updates, customer support and the reports your team uses every day.

**Disclosure:** Gavior designs and develops e-commerce websites. Use this guide to compare our offer with hosted platforms, freelancers and other agencies.

## Define the commerce model

Before selecting technology, document:

- Number and type of products
- Variants, bundles and subscriptions
- Serviceable locations and shipping rules
- Payment methods
- Tax and invoice requirements
- Inventory source of truth
- Returns and cancellation process
- Marketplace, ERP or CRM integrations
- Who manages products after launch

A store with twenty simple products is a different system from a multi-warehouse catalogue with complex pricing.

## Hosted platform or custom build?

A hosted platform can be the best choice when standard catalogue, payment and fulfilment features cover the business. Custom development becomes more relevant when the buying workflow, product configuration or integrations create genuine differentiation.

Ask the provider to compare total ownership cost: subscription, transaction fees, apps/plugins, custom development, maintenance and internal operating effort.

## Treat payment scope carefully

The safest implementation normally keeps sensitive card handling inside a reputable payment provider’s hosted or tokenised flow. PCI SSC publishes [e-commerce security guidance](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/document_library/) for protecting payment environments. Your exact compliance responsibility depends on the architecture and payment provider; an agency should not casually promise that a plugin makes the entire business compliant.

## Search visibility requires product structure

Google supports [Product structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product) to help it understand information such as price, availability and reviews when the page meets applicable requirements. Structured data confirms visible content; it does not replace useful category pages, unique product information or crawlable internal links.

Plan redirects before changing existing product URLs. Removing indexed pages without a map can erase accumulated search value.

## Measure the complete funnel

At minimum, define events for product view, add to cart, checkout start, purchase and key errors. Revenue reporting should reconcile with the payment or order system rather than relying only on a browser analytics event.

## Red flags

- Platform selected before catalogue and operations discovery.
- Payment compliance guaranteed without a responsibility discussion.
- Desktop-first design for a mobile-heavy audience.
- Product migration treated as simple copy-paste.
- No redirect plan for an existing store.
- No failed-payment, out-of-stock or refund flows.
- The agency owns store, domain or analytics accounts.
