Implementing AI automation doesn't require reinventing your company infrastructure. The highest ROI comes from embedding practical intelligence into existing business procedures. Here are four battle-tested examples.

## Example 1: Construction & Real Estate Bill-of-Quantities (BOQ) Parsing
In construction, estimating project costs from complex blueprints and vendor PDFs takes days. AI workflow pipelines extract quantities, line items, and pricing tables directly into ERP software in minutes, reducing bidding turnarounds by 80%.

## Example 2: Restaurant & Retail Inventory Sync
Retail and restaurant groups struggle with multi-channel stock sync. AI agents monitor supplier price shifts, predict ingredient demand based on foot-traffic trends, and automatically generate purchase orders before inventory drops below threshold levels.

## Example 3: Automated Legal & Vendor Contract Auditing
Legal teams spend hundreds of hours reviewing standard non-disclosure agreements, vendor contracts, and compliance docs. Automated contract pipelines flag non-standard indemnity clauses, risk scoring, and missing terms instantly.

## Example 4: Intelligent IT Incident Triage & Resolution
DevOps and IT support teams receive hundreds of alerts daily. AI triage tools ingest system logs, cross-reference stack traces with previous resolution tickets, and recommend exact bug fixes or trigger auto-healing scripts.

## Building Your First AI Workflow Pipeline

To implement durable automation:
1. Map your end-to-end process from input trigger to output store.
2. Select robust APIs and deterministic validation logic around LLM outputs.
3. Partner with experienced engineers who understand enterprise integration.
