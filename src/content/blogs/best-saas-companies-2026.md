## What Defines the Best SaaS Companies?

Before diving into specific names, it is crucial to understand what separates a good SaaS product from an industry leader. The very best platforms share three common traits:
1. **Frictionless Onboarding:** They minimize the time-to-value for new users.
2. **Scalable Architecture:** They handle massive influxes of data without performance degradation.
